NARCO MUSIC FILES

The player is configured for three local MP3 files. Place downloaded MP3 files in this folder using these exact names:
- track-1.mp3 — Cinematic Ambient Atmosphere
  https://pixabay.com/music/ambient-cinematic-ambient-atmosphere-463222/
- track-2.mp3 — Ambient Cinematic
  https://pixabay.com/music/ambient-ambient-cinematic-628167/
- track-3.mp3 — Soundscape
  https://pixabay.com/music/ambient-soundscape-338308/

Download the MP3 files from their Pixabay pages under the Pixabay Content License. Keep the filenames above unchanged. Then upload index.html and the entire music folder to the same hosting directory.

Note: The player UI is included, but MP3 audio files are not bundled in this archive because the source pages did not expose a verified direct MP3 download URL in this build environment. Until the three MP3 files are added, the controls will show but tracks will not play. Browser autoplay restrictions mean the visitor may need to tap Play once.
